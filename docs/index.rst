Common Issues Supported
=======================

Login and Account Problems
--------------------------

Support can help if you experience:

* Forgotten passwords
* Account verification issues
* Login errors
* Authentication failures
* Locked accounts

---------------------

If your VPN fails to connect, support may recommend checking:

* Firewall settings
* VPN protocols

Before Contacting Support
=========================

To speed up the support process, gather the following information:

* Registered email address
* Product version
* Operating system
* Device model
* Error message
* Screenshots (if available)
* Subscription details

Providing accurate information helps customer service diagnose issues more efficiently.

Troubleshooting Before Contacting Support
=========================================

Many VPN problems can be resolved by:

* Restarting the application
* Restarting the device
* Updating the software
* Checking the internet connection
* Reinstalling the VPN application
* Verifying account credentials

If the issue persists, contacting customer service is recommended.

Account Recovery
================

If you cannot access your account, customer service can assist with:

* Password reset guidance
* Account verification
* Subscription recovery
* License restoration

Always use the email address associated with your Bitdefender account when requesting assistance.

Customer service can answer questions regarding:

Security Best Practices
=======================

To protect your BitdefenderVPN account:

* Use a strong, unique password.
* Keep your software updated.
* Do not share account credentials.
* Monitor authorized devices regularly.
* Only contact official support channels.

Following these practices helps reduce the risk of unauthorized access.
